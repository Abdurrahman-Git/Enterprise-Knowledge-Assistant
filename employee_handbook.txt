TechCorp Employee Handbook 2024
================================

COMPANY OVERVIEW
----------------
TechCorp was founded in 2010 by Sarah Chen and Marcus Rivera in Colombo, Sri Lanka.
We are a B2B SaaS company serving over 200 enterprise clients worldwide.
Our mission is to simplify HR operations through intelligent automation.
As of 2024, we employ 500 people across 3 offices: Colombo, Singapore, and London.

WORKING HOURS
-------------
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Flexible working arrangements can be requested from your direct manager.
Remote work is available for all roles not requiring on-site presence.
Core hours (when everyone must be available) are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

LEAVE POLICY
------------
Annual Leave: All full-time employees receive 21 days of paid annual leave per year.
Leave accrues monthly at 1.75 days per month from your first day.
Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the next year.

Sick Leave: Employees receive 14 days of paid sick leave annually.
For sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required.

Maternity Leave: 84 days (12 weeks) of fully paid maternity leave.
Paternity Leave: 14 days (2 weeks) of fully paid paternity leave.
Parental leave can begin up to 2 weeks before the expected date of birth.

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
-------------------
Performance reviews are conducted twice per year: in June and December.
The review process includes self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback.
Performance is rated on a 5-point scale:
  1 - Does Not Meet Expectations
  2 - Partially Meets Expectations  
  3 - Meets Expectations
  4 - Exceeds Expectations
  5 - Outstanding

Salary increments are tied to performance ratings and are effective January 1st each year.
Employees rated 4 or 5 are eligible for the annual bonus program.

BENEFITS
--------
Health Insurance: Full medical, dental, and vision coverage for employee and dependents.
Provident Fund: Company contributes 12% of basic salary to EPF.
Training Budget: Each employee receives LKR 150,000 per year for professional development.
Gym Allowance: LKR 5,000 per month reimbursement for gym membership.
Meal Subsidy: Subsidized lunch available at the office cafeteria.

REMOTE WORK POLICY
------------------
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing 6 months of employment.
Remote work requests must be approved by the direct manager in advance.
Employees are responsible for maintaining a professional workspace at home.
The company provides a one-time LKR 50,000 home office setup allowance.

CODE OF CONDUCT
---------------
All employees must maintain professional conduct and treat colleagues with respect.
Harassment of any kind — sexual, racial, or otherwise — is strictly prohibited.
Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action including termination.
Concerns should be reported to HR or through the anonymous reporting hotline: 0800-TECHCORP.

IT AND SECURITY POLICY
-----------------------
Company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times.
Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and use a password manager.
Sharing of credentials is strictly prohibited.
Any suspected security incidents must be reported to the IT Security team within 2 hours.
